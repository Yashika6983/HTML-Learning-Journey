Who we are

A place to learn, practise and grow.

N2P Computer Institute is a computer training centre in Sampla, Haryana, affiliated with RGCSM. We help learners build useful digital knowledge through structured lessons and hands-on practice.

Our mission

To make computer education approachable and practical, so every learner can use technology with confidence in education, work and everyday life.
